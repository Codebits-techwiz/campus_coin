# 11 — Human-Readable Text Sections

This document aggregates all AI fallback templates, seeded strings, and user-facing copy generated by the system. This allows content editors to review all copy without needing to read source code.

## 1. Tips Engine Fallback Templates
Used in `services/tipsEngineService.js` if an Admin has not created an active `TipTemplate` in the database.

- **Spending Spike**:
  `Your {category} spending is >120% of your 3-month baseline. Cutting back could save up to ${excess}.`
- **Budget Warning**:
  `You have used over 80% of your {category} budget. You have ${remaining} left.`
- **Savings Goal at Risk**:
  `Your total expenses exceed your monthly allowance baseline by ${deficit}. Review non-essential spending.`
- **Default Good Behavior** *(Always appended if no rules are triggered)*:
  `Great budget tracking! Keep logging your daily meals and coffee runs to discover new saving opportunities.`

---

## 2. Monthly Insights Fallback Texts
Used in `services/aiInsightsService.js` when the Gemini LLM API is unavailable, fails, or has no API key configured.

- **Major Anomaly (Category Spike > 15%)**:
  *Summary*: `In {targetMonth}, your spending in {highestSpikeCategory} rose sharply by {percentage}% compared to your 3-month average.`
  *Tip*: `Try setting a weekly cap of $25 on {highestSpikeCategory} to get your spending back on track.`

- **Stable Spending**:
  *Summary*: `In {targetMonth}, your spending remained stable across your primary categories without major anomalies.`
  *Tip*: `Keep up the good habit! Try allocating your extra savings into your monthly goal baseline.`

- **No Data Available**:
  *Summary*: `No transactions recorded yet for {targetMonth}. Log your daily expenses to unlock monthly insights.`
  *Tip*: `Start logging your canteen and transport entries to see personalized trend summaries.`

---

## 3. Mandatory Disclaimer
*Appended to ALL AI insights, regardless of whether they were generated by the LLM or the fallback template.*

`Notice: This insight is an advisory suggestion generated based on your spending patterns, not certified financial advice.`
